# Petrobras 3W compatibility with AETHER Field

Generated by `make gate2-reports` from [the compatibility contract](../../data_contracts/semantic_mappings/3w_aether_compatibility.yaml). The contract is also seeded into `domain_compatibility_assessments`, where it controls scenario mappings.

> AETHER Field is a fictional industrial production environment. Public industrial datasets have been used for source-grounded experiments, while cross-source asset associations and operational scenarios are explicitly simulated.

## Conclusions

- No 3W variable or event class is directly compatible with an AETHER onshore gas well.
- 3W stays a separately identified offshore benchmark for detection methods.
- The database rejects a scenario mapping for any event class assessed INCOMPATIBLE or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
- AETHER telemetry for AETHER-FAULT-001 should come from a future physics-constrained synthetic capability labeled SYNTHETIC (ADR 0010).

**Status of this assessment:** engineering judgment from published definitions, version 1, dated 2026-11-05. It wasn't validated by a field engineer, a hydraulic calculation, or measurements from a gas well.

## What was compared

**Source (3W):**

- 3W README describes "offshore oil wells" and "subsea systems".
- dataset.ini names subsea Xmas-tree measurements, gas lift, a service pump, and a downhole safety valve.
- dataset.ini gives no pressure reference type, and no well geometry, fluid composition, or water depth.

**Target (a fictional AETHER well), as declared in the facility configuration:**

- Environment: onshore_surface_wellhead. Fluid: gas_dominant. Lift: natural_flow.
- Has: production_choke, production_master_valve, production_wing_valve, production_shutdown_valve.
- Doesn't have: gas_lift, downhole_safety_valve, permanent_downhole_gauge, service_pump, service_line, subsea_tree, riser.
- Not yet defined: annulus_valve_arrangement, gas_composition, water_chemistry.

These are design assumptions about a fictional asset. Choosing different equipment would change some results, so they're recorded rather than implied.

## Requirements for applying telemetry to an experimental asset

| ID | Category | Requirement | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-ENV | environment | Telemetry location exists in the target environment | Onshore surface wellhead | Seabed temperature, hydrostatic head, and riser hydraulics have no onshore equivalent. |
| REQ-FLUID | operating_condition | Producing fluid regime is comparable | Gas-dominant | Gas is compressible and shows strong expansion cooling. Oil-dominant multiphase signals do not scale to it. |
| REQ-LIFT | operating_condition | Lift method matches | Natural flow with no gas lift | Gas lift drives annulus pressure and creates its own instabilities. |
| REQ-EQUIP | equipment | The instrumented equipment exists on the target | See equipment_assumptions in config/facility/aether_field.yaml | A signal from equipment the target lacks can't describe that target. |
| REQ-REF | semantics | Pressure reference type is known and matches | GAUGE | 3W reference types are unknown. Unknown is not treated as gauge. |
| REQ-LOC | semantics | Measurement point is physically equivalent | Same point in the flow path | Similar names don't imply the same physical point. |
| REQ-MAP | governance | Any association is experimental and versioned | scenario_mappings with REPLAYED or SYNTHETIC provenance | An experimental link must never become an observed one. |

## Event classes

### Directly compatible

None.

### Conditionally compatible

**`RAPID_PRODUCTIVITY_LOSS`**

Productivity can fall quickly in a gas well too, so the category carries over. The 3W signal comes from oil-well measurements, so only the category and qualitative pattern could.

Conditions:

- A gas-well mechanism is identified and documented.
- The signal is expressed in measurements AETHER has, not subsea or downhole tags.
- Any use is labeled experimental, with no transfer of magnitudes.

**`QUICK_RESTRICTION_IN_PCK`**

The closest fit. Restricting a choke should raise upstream and lower downstream pressure in either fluid. Magnitudes and timing won't transfer. Only 6 real instances from 2 wells are available.

Conditions:

- The target well has an adjustable production choke.
- Pressure tags are shown equivalent in flow-path position, with reference types established.
- Only the direction of change is carried over, because compressible flow changes magnitudes.
- Any use is labeled experimental.

### Incompatible

**`NORMAL`**

Normal operation of an offshore oil well says nothing about normal operation of an onshore gas well. Usable only inside the 3W benchmark.

**`ABRUPT_INCREASE_OF_BSW`**

BSW is measured in an oil stream. Water problems in a gas well, such as liquid loading, are a different mechanism with a different signature.

**`SPURIOUS_CLOSURE_OF_DHSV`**

Requires a downhole safety valve, which AETHER wells don't have.

**`SEVERE_SLUGGING`**

Associated with liquid-rich flow in flowline and riser systems. AETHER has no riser. Any gathering-line liquid behavior would need its own model.

**`HYDRATE_IN_SERVICE_LINE`**

Requires a service line, which AETHER wells don't have.

### Insufficient evidence

**`FLOW_INSTABILITY`**

Gas wells can also show unstable flow, but dataset.ini gives only a name and no mechanism. The signature can't be shown to be equivalent.

**`SCALING_IN_PCK`**

Scale needs a particular water chemistry. AETHER has no fluid chemistry, so occurrence and rate can't be supported.

**`HYDRATE_IN_PRODUCTION_LINE`**

Hydrates can form in gas systems, but only inside a pressure and temperature envelope set by gas composition. AETHER has no composition, and subsea cooling differs from onshore conditions.

## Variables

### Directly compatible

None.

### Conditionally compatible

**`timestamp`**

A 1-second time index is generic, but absolute times from the source mean nothing at a fictional well.

Conditions:

- Use relative time only, because no time zone is stated.
- Keep original timestamps with the source record.

**`ABER-CKP`** (Production choke opening)

A production choke exists on an onshore gas well. Percent opening depends on trim and flow characteristic, so the same percent doesn't imply the same restriction.

Conditions:

- The target well has an adjustable production choke.
- Treat the value as an operating state, not transferable hydraulics.

**`ESTADO-M1`** (Production master valve)

Surface wellheads also have a production master valve with the same open-or-closed function, though the subsea actuation context differs.

Conditions:

- Compare valve state only.
- The target wellhead declares a production master valve.

**`ESTADO-SDV-P`** (Production shutdown valve)

Production shutdown is a common function, but dataset.ini doesn't state where this valve sits in the flow path.

Conditions:

- Compare valve state only.
- The target declares a production shutdown valve at a stated location.

**`ESTADO-W1`** (Production wing valve)

Surface trees have a production wing valve with the same function, though the subsea context differs.

Conditions:

- Compare valve state only.
- The target wellhead declares a production wing valve.

**`P-ANULAR`** (Well annulus)

Annulus pressure exists on both, but in a gas-lifted well the injected gas drives it, and AETHER has no gas lift. The reference type is unknown.

Conditions:

- Reference type is established.
- Gas lift influence is excluded from the instance.

**`P-JUS-CKP`** (Downstream of production choke)

Pressure downstream of a choke exists on both, but values depend on the flowline, fluid, and choke location. dataset.ini doesn't state where the choke sits.

Conditions:

- Reference type is established.
- Choke position in the flow path is comparable.
- Fluid regime is accounted for rather than assumed.

**`P-MON-CKP`** (Upstream of production choke)

Can't be equated with onshore wellhead pressure without knowing how much flow path lies between the well and this choke. Conditional only if that is shown.

Conditions:

- Reference type is established.
- Choke position in the flow path is comparable.
- Fluid regime is accounted for rather than assumed.

### Incompatible

**`class`**

A per-observation label for 3W's own offshore events. It describes a real or generated 3W instance and cannot be a label for an AETHER asset.

**`ABER-CKGL`** (Gas lift choke opening)

AETHER wells have no gas lift, so this equipment doesn't exist on the target.

**`ESTADO-DHSV`** (Downhole safety valve)

AETHER wells declare no downhole safety valve.

**`ESTADO-PXO`** (Pig-crossover valve)

A subsea production-system valve with no declared equivalent on an AETHER wellhead.

**`ESTADO-SDV-GL`** (Gas lift shutdown valve)

Depends on gas lift, which AETHER wells don't have.

**`ESTADO-XO`** (Crossover valve)

A subsea tree valve linking production and annulus paths, with no declared AETHER equivalent.

**`P-JUS-BS`** (Downstream of service pump)

AETHER wells declare no service pump.

**`P-JUS-CKGL`** (Downstream of gas lift choke)

Depends on gas lift.

**`P-MON-CKGL`** (Upstream of gas lift choke)

Depends on gas lift.

**`PT-P`** (Subsea Xmas-tree production line downstream of production wing valve)

A seabed measurement point. Depth and subsea conditions separate it from any surface point, so it must not map to onshore wellhead pressure.

**`P-TPT`** (Subsea Xmas-tree temperature and pressure transducer)

A seabed measurement point on a subsea tree, so it must not map to onshore wellhead pressure.

**`QBS`** (Service pump)

AETHER wells declare no service pump.

**`QGL`** (Gas lift line)

Depends on gas lift.

**`T-TPT`** (Subsea Xmas-tree temperature and pressure transducer)

A seabed temperature set by the marine environment, which onshore wells don't have.

### Insufficient evidence

**`state`**

dataset.ini calls it well operational status without defining its values for other well types.

**`ESTADO-M2`** (Annulus master valve)

Onshore annulus access differs from a subsea tree's annulus master valve, and AETHER's casing valve arrangement isn't defined yet.

**`ESTADO-W2`** (Annulus wing valve)

AETHER's annulus valve arrangement isn't defined yet.

**`P-MON-SDV-P`** (Upstream of production shutdown valve)

The valve's location in the flow path isn't published, so the measurement point can't be matched.

**`P-PDG`** (Permanent downhole gauge)

AETHER declares no downhole gauge, and no well depth or fluid column is available to relate downhole pressure to the surface.

**`T-JUS-CKP`** (Downstream of production choke)

Gas shows strong cooling across a choke while oil-dominant flow behaves differently. Without fluid composition, the signal can't be related to a gas well.

**`T-MON-CKP`** (Upstream of production choke)

Reflects heat loss along an undocumented offshore flow path and an unknown fluid.

**`T-PDG`** (Permanent downhole gauge)

AETHER declares no downhole gauge, and no well depth or geothermal data is available.

## How this is enforced

| Rule | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| An offshore tag can't map to an onshore measurement | `sensor_tag_mappings` foreign keys require the same equipment class |
| An unknown pressure reference can't become gauge | The same keys require the same reference type |
| An incompatible event can't reach an AETHER asset | `scenario_mapping_guard` trigger |
| A conditional event needs evidence for every condition | `scenario_mapping_guard` trigger |
| A mapping can't be OBSERVED or overwrite source identity | Check constraint, composite key, and an immutability trigger |

Tests that attempt each of these are in `tests/provenance/test_database_invariants.py`.

## Consequence for AETHER-FAULT-001

The original scenario replayed a 3W event against a fictional well. That is no longer the default path:

1. 3W stays a separate benchmark for detection methods, evaluated on its own terms.
2. AETHER telemetry should come from a future physics-constrained synthetic capability, labeled `SYNTHETIC` (ADR 0010). Gate 2 doesn't build it.
3. `QUICK_RESTRICTION_IN_PCK` and `RAPID_PRODUCTIVITY_LOSS` remain possible qualitative references, but only with evidence for every listed condition.

## Limitations

- `dataset.ini` gives names, units, and short descriptions. It doesn't publish well geometry, water depth, fluid composition, or choke location.
- Mechanism statements rest on general engineering knowledge and were not checked against a published gas-well field study.
- AETHER's equipment is chosen, not observed, so this compares 3W with a declared design rather than with a real facility.
