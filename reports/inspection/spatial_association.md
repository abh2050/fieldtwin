# Spatial association: proximity, not attribution

Generated by `pipelines/inspection/reports.py`. Rules: `config/inspection/gate6_association.yaml`, version 1.

## What was compared

455 Carbon Mapper methane plume origins against 645 registry gas wells whose coordinates Gate 2 accepts for spatial analysis (681 in the registry; QUARANTINED 1, UNRESOLVED_DATUM 35, USABLE 645).

A plume and a well are linked as `EMISSION_NEAR` when the plume origin lies within 250 m of the well. Every link is `UNVERIFIED`, carries no confidence value, and is DERIVED. The database refuses to mark one verified or to turn it into `SOURCE_ATTRIBUTED_TO`.

**Disclosure:** the distribution of plume-to-well distances and a random-point baseline were looked at once, during source inspection, before the radius was fixed. The other radii are reported so the choice can be judged.

## Proximity against chance

How often a plume origin lies within each radius of any registry well, beside how often a uniformly random point in the study area does (20,000 points, seed 20261110).

| Radius | Plumes with a well inside | Share | Random points | Above chance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 m | 65 of 455 | 14.3% | 3.2% | +11.1 points |
| 250 m | 158 of 455 | 34.7% | 15.9% | +18.8 points |
| 500 m | 275 of 455 | 60.4% | 41.9% | +18.5 points |
| 1000 m | 339 of 455 | 74.5% | 69.5% | +5.1 points |

Plume origins sit closer to registry wells than random points do. Relative to chance the difference is largest at 100 m (4.4 times the random share), and it has nearly vanished at 1000 m, where most of the study area is within reach of some well. This says plumes tend to occur where wells and their surrounding equipment are, not that any well is a source. Plumes repeat at persistent sources, so these shares are not independent trials and no interval is given for them.

## Why proximity can't name a source

- **Several candidates.** Of the 158 plumes with a well within 250 m, 45 have exactly one candidate, 72 have two to four, and 41 have five or more.
- **An incomplete registry.** The registry holds gas wells only. Oil wells, tank batteries, compressors, flares, and pipelines are absent, so the nearest registered well isn't necessarily the nearest infrastructure. One chip viewed during development shows a tank battery beside the registered pad; the chips have not otherwise been reviewed.
- **Uncertain locations.** Carbon Mapper states that plume representations may not precisely match infrastructure on the ground. OCD describes its well locations as approximate.
- **Wind and time.** A plume origin is an instantaneous estimate from one overpass.

Nearest-link distances among linked plumes, quartiles: 77, 122, 184 m. Links at other radii: 100 m: 141, 500 m: 1534, 1000 m: 4485.

## What this supports

A statement of the form "a plume origin was detected N metres from this registered well, and K other registered wells are also within the radius." Nothing in Gate 6 supports a leak at a well, an emission source, an equipment condition, or any link to AETHER Field.
