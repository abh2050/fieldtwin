# AETHER synthetic benchmark

Generated by `make gate5-reports` from `reports/experiments/results/`.

> **Evidence class: SYNTHETIC experiment.** AETHER Field is fictional. This telemetry comes from an uncalibrated, simplified model, not a validated engineering simulator, so these results say nothing about detection on any real gas system.

## Dataset

272 cases of 7 days at 300 s sampling, generated under the process guard with outcomes {'OK': 272}. Dataset SHA-256 `52e38b27fbea6443...`.

| Split | Cases |
|---|---:|
| held_out | 112 |
| test | 32 |
| train | 96 |
| validation | 32 |

Every case varies well deliverability, sensor noise, compressor capacity, and a stepped suction setpoint, which is normal operating variation no detector is told about. Fault cases vary severity, onset, and ramp. Planned well shut-ins and communication dropouts are normal operation, not anomalies. Seeds and parameter sets never repeat across splits, and fault truth is stored apart from telemetry.

Held-out regimes, never used in fitting or threshold setting: higher and lower severity, unseen assets (GS-TRUNK, C-03 and C-04, Pad B sensors), noise 2 to 3 times higher, deliverability outside the training band, onset earlier or later than in training, compressor setpoints outside training with one unit offline, and faults with an overlapping dropout. Setpoint steps of 2% can reach just inside the training range's edge.

## Limits

- The simulator is uncalibrated and quasi-steady, so the benchmark rewards detecting this model's behavior.
- A linear model fitting this well partly reflects that the simulator is smooth and its faults have consistent signatures.
- Per-category counts are small: 4 events each in-regime, and 2 per category in each held-out regime.
