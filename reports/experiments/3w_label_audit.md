# 3W label audit

Generated by `make gate5-reports` from `reports/experiments/results/`.

> **Evidence class: OBSERVED benchmark.** Petrobras 3W is recorded offshore oil-well data, labeled by its publisher. It is an independent benchmark and was never measured at AETHER Field. It covers a deterministic 498-instance subset of 3W 2.0.0, and these results don't establish field-validated detection performance.

## Result

All 498 acquired instances were audited, and no source label was changed. **43 real instances filed under class 9, `HYDRATE_IN_SERVICE_LINE`, contain no event-labeled row.** They hold only normal and unlabeled rows. They are graded ambiguous and kept out of every primary metric, as neither events nor normal operation.

## The 43 class-9 instances

- **Size:** 611 hours, of which 568 are labeled normal and 43 are unlabeled.
- **Wells:** 11, at most 8 instances on one well.
- **Documentation checked:** `dataset.ini`, `3W_DATASET_STRUCTURE.md`, the README, and `paper/paper.md` at the pinned commit, plus a search of the repository's issues. None explains these instances. The two doctoral theses in the repository were not read.
- **Decision:** with nothing authoritative to resolve their meaning, they stay ambiguous. They are not scored as positive events, and they are not relabeled as normal.

Sensitivity of each method to scoring them either way is in [robustness](robustness.md).

## Subset

These 498 instances are a deterministic subset of the 2,228 files in 3W 2.0.0, chosen by sorted file name in Gate 1. It keeps every real event instance except class 4, capped at 60 of 343, and 100 of 594 real normal instances. Sorting by name favors lower well numbers and earlier dates, so normal operation covers only 9 wells.
