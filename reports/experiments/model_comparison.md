# Model comparison

Generated by `make gate5-reports` from `reports/experiments/results/`.

> **Two evidence classes, never pooled.** 3W results are OBSERVED benchmark evidence from recorded offshore data. AETHER results are SYNTHETIC experiments on a fictional, uncalibrated model. Neither establishes field-validated detection performance.

All methods use the same splits, the same causal features, preprocessing fitted on training data only, and the same operating point. Parameters were fixed in advance and not tuned. Selection follows the declared rule: within both alarm limits, the false-alarm budget, best event skill, then the simplest tied method.

## Selection

| Track | Data used | Preferred | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3W (OBSERVED) | Development wells | Static limits | 1 method(s) met both alarm limits. static_threshold had the best event skill among them (-0.001). 1 method(s) have skill intervals including it, and static_threshold is the simplest of them. |
| AETHER (SYNTHETIC) | In-regime test | Gradient boosting | 1 method(s) met both alarm limits. hist_gradient_boosting had the best event skill among them (0.500). 1 method(s) have skill intervals including it, and hist_gradient_boosting is the simplest of them. |

## Tradeoffs

| Method | 3W development skill | 3W holdout skill | AETHER in-regime skill | AETHER held-out skill | 3W development false alarms per hour | Explainability | Maintainability | p95 window latency, 3W / AETHER (ms) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Static limits | -0.001 | 0.025 | 0.379 | -0.021 | 0.002 | High: one fixed band per sensor | Low: fixed limits, refit only to recompute bands | 0.15 / 0.06 |
| Rolling deviation | 0.127 | 0.042 | 0.286 | 0.222 | 0.067 | High: names the sensor that moved from its own past | Low: no training labels | 0.84 / 1.23 |
| Logistic regression | 0.156 | 0.200 | 0.567 | 0.179 | 0.013 | Medium: linear weights on named features | Medium: retrain when sensors change | 0.41 / 0.46 |
| Isolation forest | -0.096 | 0.033 | -0.014 | -0.023 | 0.035 | Low: path-length score with no direct reason | Medium: retrain on fresh normal data | 4.24 / 4.12 |
| Gradient boosting | -0.051 | -0.067 | 0.500 | 0.329 | 0.019 | Low: 150 trees | Higher: retrain and recheck calibration | 4.17 / 1.73 |

Explainability and maintainability are judgments about each method's form, not measurements. Robustness is in [robustness](robustness.md), and latency in [inference performance](inference_performance.md).

## Reading the two tracks together

The tracks answer different questions and are never averaged. A method winning on AETHER shows it can detect this simulator's faults. Only the 3W results bear on recorded industrial data, and only for offshore oil wells at a fixed operating point without per-well adaptation.
