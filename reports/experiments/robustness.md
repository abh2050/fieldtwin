# Robustness

Generated by `make gate5-reports` from `reports/experiments/results/`.

> **Two evidence classes, never pooled.** 3W results are OBSERVED benchmark evidence from recorded offshore data. AETHER results are SYNTHETIC experiments on a fictional, uncalibrated model. Neither establishes field-validated detection performance.

## Missing data

Models and thresholds stay fixed while inputs are degraded. Each cell shows event recall and false alarms per hour.

Perturbations: a quarter of present sensors removed from each instance; 20% of readings missing at random; and all readings lost for 10 minutes of each hour on 3W, or for 1 hour in 10 on AETHER.
